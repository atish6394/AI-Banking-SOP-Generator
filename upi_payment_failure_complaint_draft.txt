# Standard Operating Procedure: UPI payment failure complaint

**Audience:** Customer support agent
**Created:** 2026-10-23
**Status:** Draft — requires review and approval

## 1. Purpose
Provide a consistent approach for handling this process.

## 2. Process details supplied
A customer reports that money was debited but the beneficiary did not receive the payment. Record the complaint, verify transaction details through approved systems, follow the approved dispute/refund process, escalate when required, and update the case status.

## 3. Procedure
1. Receive the request and record the case in the approved case-management system.
2. Confirm required information using approved internal procedures.
3. Review case details and status in authorized systems.
4. Follow the organization's approved procedure; do not promise an outcome before verification.
5. Escalate unresolved or unusual cases to the designated team.
6. Record actions taken and update the case status.
7. Close the case only when approved closure criteria are met.

## 4. Roles
- Case handler: record information and document actions.
- Process owner: review exceptions and approve decisions where required.

## 5. Escalation
- Escalate cases that cannot be resolved using the approved process.
- Confirm applicable targets and escalation paths with the process owner.

## 6. Verification checklist
- [ ] Case recorded
- [ ] Information checked through authorized systems
- [ ] Approved procedure followed
- [ ] Escalation completed if required
- [ ] Actions and status documented
- [ ] Draft reviewed by process owner
